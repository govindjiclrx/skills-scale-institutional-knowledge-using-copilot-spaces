# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides a quick overview of the OctoAcme project management process and links to the key process documents in this folder.

## Project management overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, repeatable lifecycle that starts with initiation, moves into planning, continues through execution and tracking, and ends with release and retrospective improvement. The process emphasizes clear ownership, small shippable increments, regular communication, practical quality checks, and ongoing learning so teams can deliver work consistently and transparently.

## How the process fits together

- **Initiation** confirms the business need, stakeholders, success metrics, and go/no-go decision.
- **Planning** turns the approved idea into a backlog, release plan, and dependency-aware delivery approach.
- **Execution and tracking** uses boards, standups, PR conventions, CI, and reporting to keep work visible and moving.
- **Risk and communication** keeps stakeholders informed and escalates blockers through a clear path.
- **Release and deployment** standardizes production rollout, verification, and rollback planning.
- **Retrospectives** capture lessons learned and turn them into tracked improvements.
- **Roles and personas** define who owns product outcomes, delivery coordination, implementation, and validation.

## Quality and collaboration

Quality is built into the process through unit, integration, and smoke testing, CI checks, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Collaboration is supported through regular PM/PdM syncs, delivery standups, milestone reviews, and stakeholder updates that keep decisions and progress visible.
