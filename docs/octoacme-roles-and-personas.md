# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the path from approved work to production release. They make sure timing, readiness, and communication are aligned across delivery and support teams.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm release readiness with Developers, QA, and Project Managers
- Coordinate deployment windows, cutover steps, and go/no-go decisions
- Track release dependencies, risks, and rollback readiness
- Publish release notes and communicate release status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and avoid last-minute surprises
- Ensure clear ownership of release activities and approvals
- Deliver predictable, well-communicated releases

### Typical Communication
- Works with Project Managers on release timelines and milestones
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on readiness and verification
- Partners with Product Managers on scope, priority, and launch messaging
- Updates stakeholders on release status and expected impacts

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy for a project or release. They define verification approach, ensure test coverage is sufficient, and confirm quality gates are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards
- Review acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Coordinate testing activities across manual and automated coverage
- Sign off on release readiness from a quality perspective

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Make quality expectations visible and measurable
- Improve confidence in delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Works closely with Developers on test planning and defect triage
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Reports quality risks and testing status to Project Managers
- Supports Release Managers with final verification before deployment

---

## Technical Writers

### Role Summary
Technical Writers turn project decisions, release details, and operational knowledge into clear documentation. They help ensure that internal and external audiences can understand what changed and how to use it.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and maintain release notes, process docs, and user-facing guidance
- Translate technical changes into clear, concise language
- Coordinate with subject matter experts to confirm accuracy
- Keep documentation updated as scope or workflows change

### Goals
- Improve clarity and reduce knowledge gaps
- Make project outputs easier to adopt and support
- Keep documentation aligned with shipped functionality and processes

### Typical Communication
- Works with Product Managers to understand scope and intended outcomes
- Coordinates with Developers and QA Leads to validate technical accuracy
- Partners with Release Managers on release notes and launch communications
- Supports Project Managers by keeping process documentation current

---

## Stakeholder Champions

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions represent the needs of a stakeholder group and help consolidate feedback, questions, and decisions. They reduce communication noise and improve alignment across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize stakeholder feedback
- Surface concerns, approvals, and decision points
- Help confirm priorities, expectations, and launch readiness
- Support communication between stakeholders and delivery teams

### Goals
- Improve decision-making speed and clarity
- Ensure stakeholder input is heard and organized
- Reduce misalignment between project teams and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Works with Project Managers on stakeholder updates and action items
- Aligns with Product Managers on priority and outcome expectations
- Supports Release Managers during launch communication
- Escalates unresolved concerns through the agreed project channels
